Q: Reflected glare off a lake is mostly…
A) Unpolarised, like direct sunlight
B) Partly polarised, vibrating mostly horizontally
C) Ultraviolet light
D) Blue light
Correct: B
Explanation: Reflection from a flat surface is partly polarised, mainly horizontally. A vertical filter blocks much of it.

Q: In the simulator, when does a polarised lens cut water glare the most?
A) Looking straight down at the water
B) Looking ahead and down at roughly Brewster's angle
C) At night
D) Always exactly the same
Correct: B
Explanation: Near Brewster's angle (about 53° from vertical for water) the reflection is most strongly polarised. The effect depends on the angle.

Q: Two lenses, one in front of the other. One is turned 90°. The overlap went almost black. What does it show?
A) Both lenses are polarised
B) Both lenses block UV
C) The lenses are category 4
D) The lenses are photochromic
Correct: A
Explanation: Two polarisers crossed at 90° block most light. It proves polarisation — nothing about UV or category.
